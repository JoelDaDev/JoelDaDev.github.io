Destruction

First Draft
663 words Goal: 5,000

A sci-fi short story about humanity's exodus from Earth, and one man's quest to save his family.

Progress: 13% complete (663 / 5,000 words)

Genre: Sci-fi thriller

Themes: Gods, kings, lies, political drama.

Synopsis:

Thirty years ago, the United World Government interrupted every signal on Earth with a single announcement: the planet was dying, and only a fraction of humanity could be saved. Hundreds of massive life ships — Arks — were already under construction, but nowhere near enough to carry the global population. Billions would be left behind.

At the time, there was hope. Scientific projections estimated nearly a century remained before Earth became uninhabitable, giving humanity time to evacuate in an orderly, if cruel, selection process.

That estimate was wrong.

Now, three decades into the evacuation effort, a second broadcast shatters what remains of global stability. Planetary systems are collapsing far faster than predicted. Humanity is informed it has four weeks before Earth becomes completely uninhabitable. The selection process is immediately reduced to randomized allocation, leaving most people with no control over their fate.

In the midst of this collapse, Josiah Martin realizes his family will not be chosen. With time running out and systems already locked into final evacuation protocols, he makes a decision that will define his survival: he will not wait for selection. He will infiltrate an Ark.

Current stage: First draft

This is my NaNoWriMo project.

Progress: 0% complete (0 / 50,000 words)

Genre: Dystopian Fantasy

Themes: Gods, kings, lies, political drama.

Synopsis: Five hundred years ago, humanity chained its gods. The world died for it. What’s left lives behind the Wall of Sanctum, on rationed water and borrowed light, while the Shade swallows everything beyond. Tobin copies the Government’s decrees. He was taken from the slums as a boy, given a new name, and taught to be grateful. When a rebel named Ves corners him in the market, Tobin threatens to turn him in. He never does. Instead, he starts reading. The histories have been scraped and rewritten. The rations keep shrinking. And beneath the city, the Engines hum like something breathing. Some lies are the only thing holding a city up.

Weekly devotional

A Word for the Week

"Finally, all of you, be like-minded, be sympathetic, love one another, be compassionate and humble."

1 Peter 3:8

We live in a world filled with hate, anger, and division. Every day we see people tearing each other down instead of lifting each other up. But God calls us to a different way — a way of compassion and humility.

He calls us to love instead of hate, to be slow to anger, and to walk in humility. When we live this way, we reflect the heart of Jesus in a world that desperately needs His light.

A new devotional, published once a week.

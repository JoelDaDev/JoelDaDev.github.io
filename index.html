Destruction First Draft

📖 663 words 🎯 Goal: 5,000

A sci-fi short story about humanity's exodous from earth, and one man's quest to save his family.

Project Overview

Progress: 13% complete (663 / 5,000 words)

Genre: Sci-fi Thriller

Themes: Gods, kings, lies, political drama.

Synopsis:
Thirty years ago, the United World Government interrupted every signal on Earth with a single announcement: the planet was dying, and only a fraction of humanity could be saved. Hundreds of massive life ships—Arks—were already under construction, but nowhere near enough to carry the global population. Billions would be left behind. At the time, there was hope. Scientific projections estimated nearly a century remained before Earth became uninhabitable, giving humanity time to evacuate in an orderly, if cruel, selection process. That estimate was wrong. Now, three decades into the evacuation effort, a second broadcast shatters what remains of global stability. Planetary systems are collapsing far faster than predicted. Humanity is informed it has four weeks before Earth becomes completely uninhabitable. The selection process is immediately reduced to randomized allocation, leaving most people with no control over their fate. In the midst of this collapse, Josiah Martin realizes his family will not be chosen. With time running out and systems already locked into final evacuation protocols, he makes a decision that will define his survival: he will not wait for selection. He will infiltrate an Ark.

Current Stage: First Draft

"Finally, all of you, be like-minded, be sympathetic, love one another, be compassionate and humble."

- 1 Peter 3:8

We live in a world filled with hate, anger, and division. Every day we see people tearing each other down instead of lifting each other up. But God calls us to a different way, a way of compassion and humility.

God calls us to love instead of hate, to be slow to anger, and to walk in humility. When we live this way, we reflect the heart of Jesus in a world that desperatly needs His light.

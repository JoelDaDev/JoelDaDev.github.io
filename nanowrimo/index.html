The book

Five hundred years ago, humanity chained its gods. The world died for it.

What's left lives behind the Wall of Sanctum, on rationed water and borrowed light, while the Shade swallows everything beyond.

Tobin copies the Government's decrees. He was taken from the slums as a boy, given a new name, and taught to be grateful. When a rebel named Ves corners him in the market, Tobin threatens to turn him in.

He never does.

Instead, he starts reading. The histories have been scraped and rewritten. The rations keep shrinking. And beneath the city, the Engines hum like something breathing.

Some lies are the only thing holding a city up.
